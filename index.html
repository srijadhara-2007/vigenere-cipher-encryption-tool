How the Vigenère Cipher Works

The Vigenère Cipher is a polyalphabetic substitution cipher. Each letter in the plaintext is shifted by an amount determined by the corresponding letter of a repeating keyword. Unlike the Caesar Cipher, the same plaintext letter can map to different ciphertext letters, making it far more resistant to frequency analysis.
